# Four seasons

Four built-in themes, written as plain theme files

Press **Shift+T** to walk from spring to winter

---

## Spring and summer

- **Spring:** cherry blossom and new leaves on a pale morning
- **Summer:** sun on sand and a deep blue sea
- Both run on the **plain** engine

---

## Autumn and winter

- **Autumn:** dark bark and maple amber
- **Winter:** a clear night over snow
- Both run on the **particles** engine, in their own colours
