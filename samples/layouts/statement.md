# Design: statement
One idea, said big


## Why markdown

Because the slides should be the easy part of a talk.


## The rule of thumb

If a slide needs more than two sentences, it is two slides.

Or a list.

---

Just one sentence, without a heading, is a statement too.


## A longer statement still fits

Statements of up to two hundred and forty characters stay statements, so a
thought that takes a breath or two in the middle still gets the big type it
deserves on the screen.
