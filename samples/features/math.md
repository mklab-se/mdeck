# The quadratic formula

The roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ are

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

and they are real when $b^2 \geq 4ac$. Prices like $5 and $10 stay text.
